# ADR 002 · Put routing in the workflow

**Status**

Proposed in the initial design.

## Context

Tests or a review can send work back. A retry must have a predictable cost.

## Decision

Use two explicit Actions rounds. The builder retries once after a failed test or build. The second round starts at design or implementation according to the published finding.

## Alternative considered

A planner could pick a fallback dynamically, but it could also route around a gate or loop.

## Consequences

The supported routes are visible in the run graph. A third review requires a new trigger.
